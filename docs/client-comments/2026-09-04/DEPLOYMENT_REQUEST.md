# What we need from CECODES to launch

Sent to the client on 2026-09-04 by WhatsApp. Short version: only the items that actually
block going online. Company list, historical data, training dates and the privacy text are
deliberately left out and will be asked for after the tool is live.

Ownership model agreed with the developer: CECODES owns and pays for the accounts, the
developer is added as a collaborator.

---

Hello! We are ready to put the tool online. I need 6 things from your side.

_1. Three accounts._ I recommend CECODES creates and owns these, so the tool belongs to you
from day one. Create each with a shared CECODES email (not personal), then invite me:

- vercel.com (where the application runs)
- supabase.com (where your data is stored)
- resend.com (sends the password emails)

Each has a free level. I will tell you the exact cost before anything is charged. Please do
not add a payment card until I confirm.

_If this part is complicated, do not worry about it._ Just tell me and I will create the three
accounts myself in the name of CECODES, set everything up, and transfer them to you at the end.
The only thing I would need from you then is a CECODES email address to register them under.

_2. The web address._ You already have a domain. Tell me the exact address you want, for
example huella.cecodes.org.co

_3. The person who manages that domain._ I do not need a password. I will send a few small
technical records and that person adds them. About 10 minutes of their time.

_4. The administrators (administradores)._ Name and email of the people at CECODES who will
manage everything. Please give me 2, not 1, so you are never locked out.

_5. The reference values_, with the source of each:

- national electricity grid factor (factor de red), per year
- average price per gallon of gasoline and of diesel, per year

Without these, electricity (Alcance 2) and transport subsidies (Alcance 3) cannot be
calculated for that year.

_6. Written confirmation of the emission factors._ We loaded the sheet you sent on
3 September 2026. Please reply confirming that this is the official version for launch. The
whole tool calculates from it, so I need your confirmation on record.

That is everything to go live. I will ask you separately about the list of member companies,
historical data and training, none of which block us.

And in general: if any of these steps is difficult on your side, tell me and I will do it for
you. It is easier for me to handle it than for us to wait.

Gracias!
